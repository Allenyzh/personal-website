TaxAgentServer is an AI-powered tax filing assistant built at a hackathon at John Abbott College in April 2025. It uses Python, Playwright and Google GenAI to automate the tax return process.

## What it does

- Automates the browser with Playwright to interact with web forms and extract data
- Uses Google Generative AI for natural language processing and tax guidance
- Chains both into an automated workflow that reduced manual tax filing time by 70%
